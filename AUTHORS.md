# Authors

Shimera is developed by **The Shimera Authors**: the maintainers and contributors
listed below. The `Copyright (C) 2025-2026 The Shimera Authors` notice found in
each source file refers collectively to these people. **Each author keeps the
copyright to their own contributions.**

Two roles are distinguished:

- **Copyright** is held by every author (maintainers and contributors), each over
  the part they wrote.
- The **right to relicense the project** is granted to the **maintainers** only.
  Under the "Licensing of Contributions" section of [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md),
  every contributor grants the maintainers a broad license over their contribution,
  including the right to change the project's license in the future. The maintainers
  are therefore the people who can decide a future license change.

## Contributors

Everyone who has contributed to Shimera. Each keeps the copyright to their own
contributions. New contributors add themselves here in the pull request of their
first contribution (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), using the format
`- Full Name ([@github-handle](https://github.com/github-handle))`.

## Name and logo

The GPL-3.0 covers the source code only. The name "Shimera" and the Shimera logo
are not licensed under the GPL: forks are welcome to reuse the code, but must not
present themselves as the official Shimera project.
